# Chão das Minas Ocas

- **Categoria:** Mapas: chão (textura contínua)
- **Salvar em:** `client/public/assets/mapas/minas-ocas-chao.png`
- **Formato:** Textura de chão contínua (repetível), opaca
- **Tamanho a pedir:** 1024x1024

## Prompt (copie e cole no ChatGPT)

```text
2D pixel art game asset in 16-bit JRPG style. Crisp hard-edged pixels, visible pixel grid, no anti-aliasing, no blur, no smooth gradients, no photorealism, no 3D render. Limited palette of about 24 colors: near-black, charcoal, deep violet and indigo as base, with small cyan, violet or amber glowing accents. Dark modern-fantasy mood. 1-pixel dark outline around every shape. Light source from the top-left. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no signature, no UI frame.

Subject: dark brown cave floor with gravel, rust stains and faint rail tracks marks.

Output: a seamless tileable ground texture seen from directly above, square 1024x1024, fully opaque, edge to edge. The left edge must match the right edge and the top edge must match the bottom edge so it repeats with no visible seam. Low contrast and no large unique landmarks, because characters must stay readable on top of it. No objects, no characters, no shadows of objects.
```

## Antes de salvar, confira

- [ ] Lado a lado, a textura repete sem emenda visível.
- [ ] Contraste baixo: os personagens continuam legíveis por cima.
- [ ] Sem objetos grandes nem personagens.
- [ ] O estilo bate com os assets já aprovados (mesma paleta e mesmo contorno).
