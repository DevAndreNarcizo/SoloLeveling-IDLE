# Golpe corpo a corpo

- **Categoria:** Efeitos
- **Salvar em:** `client/public/assets/efeitos/golpe.png`
- **Formato:** Folha 2×2 com quatro quadros de animação, fundo transparente
- **Tamanho a pedir:** 1024x1024

## Prompt (copie e cole no ChatGPT)

```text
Professional 16-bit pixel art for a top-down 2D RPG, in the style of classic JRPG overworld art. Characters and monsters about three heads tall (not super chibi). Detailed shading, muted dark fantasy palette with violet, cyan or amber glowing accents. Crisp hard-edged pixels, 1-pixel dark outline, no anti-aliasing. No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no UI frame.

Subject: a white-violet crescent slash arc, four animation frames in reading order: thin start, full arc, fading arc, small sparks.

Output: one sheet, square 1024x1024 canvas, divided into an exact 2 columns x 2 rows grid of equal 512x512 cells, no grid lines. Four animation frames of the same effect in reading order (top-left, top-right, bottom-left, bottom-right), each centered in its cell. Fully transparent background (PNG with alpha). No ground shadow, no floor, no backdrop.
```

## Antes de salvar, confira

- [ ] Quatro quadros na ordem de leitura.
- [ ] Cada quadro centralizado na sua célula.
- [ ] O fundo é transparente.
- [ ] O estilo bate com os assets já aprovados (mesma paleta e mesmo contorno).
